What was asked

"I applied the game but I didn't see much working — can you test it yourself and make it an exact replica of how everything works? I've never played so I have no idea."

Follow-up decisions taken during the session:

  • Priority: interface fidelity first, then bug fixes, then a guided intro.
  • 3D world: match OSRS closely (camera, lighting, ground texturing).

The headline finding

The engine was already in good shape. Combat maths, every skill, pathfinding, NPC aggression, death and respawn with item-keeping, banking, shops, dialogue, prayer drain, magic, and the social system were all working and faithful to the real formulas. "Nothing worked" came down to three things:

  1. The Skills, Prayer and Magic tabs drew plain coloured circles. The spellbook was 34 identical blobs and the prayer book 29 grey discs — genuinely unusable, which makes the whole game feel broken even though the logic underneath was fine.
  2. The camera was about twice as far out as the real client, so everything looked small and remote.
  3. There was no guidance whatsoever — you spawn in a field with no idea what to do, which matters a lot for someone who has never played.

Real bugs found and fixed

fixManually cast spells used the weapon's melee range

playerCanReach() only granted the 10-tile spell range when a spell was autocast. A hand-cast spell fell back to player.attackRange, which is 1 while unarmed, so clicking a spell then a target more than one tile away failed silently — no message, no rune use, nothing. This is why magic looked completely dead.

Fixed in src/server/combat.ts: added SPELL_RANGE = 10 and an explicit range override, which castCombatSpell() now passes.

fixExamine walked you to the target and could fail

Object Examine went through walkAndDo(), so examining anything far away made you trudge over and often ended in "I can't reach that!". In RuneScape examine is instant from anywhere. handleObjectAction() in src/server/actions.ts now answers Examine before any pathing. (NPC and ground-item examine were already instant.)

fixThe client could not tell whether Run was enabled

The run orb and the Options toggle both guessed from runEnergy > 0, so they lied whenever run was switched off with energy left. Added runOn to the sync packet (src/shared/net.ts) and wired it through state.ts and the orb / options tab.

Interface work

newsrc/client/ui/glyphs.ts

Procedurally drawn interface art, cached into small offscreen canvases, in the same chunky flat style as the existing item sprites in icons.ts:

  • 23 skill icons — crossed swords, heart, pickaxe, flexed arm, boot, hammer, shield, potion, fish, bow, purse, cooking pot, praying hands, needle & thread, flame, stars, fletching knife, axe in a log, rune disc, horned skull, sprout, hammer & saw, box trap.
  • 29 prayer icons — grouped into families via PRAYER_FAMILY (defence / strength / attack / ranged / magic / protect-*, etc.). Each family shares a base glyph and gains detail with its tier, so the book reads at a glance like the real one.
  • 34 spell icons — combat spells take their shape from the tier (Strike = dart, Bolt = forked bolt, Blast = burst, Wave = three crests) and their colour from the element. Teleports are rune tablets with a city-coloured swirl, curses are drain arrows, enchants are gems, alchemy is coins over a flame.

artTab readouts, framing and layout

  • Prayer, Magic and Getting Started tabs each gained an OSRS-style readout strip naming whatever the cursor is over.
  • The spellbook strip shows the spell's level, xp, and rune costs coloured green or red by whether you are carrying enough.
  • New frame() helper draws the chunky bordered panel with corner studs; used by the bank, shop, make-amount and equipment-stats interfaces.
  • Shop slots highlight on hover, grey out when out of stock, and a bottom strip shows name / price / stock.
  • Tab strip: inventory is now a backpack and magic is a wizard hat rather than generic shapes.

3D world

artCamera matched to the real client

The framing was measured rather than guessed, using a new window.game.tileAt(sx, sy) hook that reports which world tile sits under a screen position.

Tiles across the 512px viewport
Before10
First attempt (distance 880)7 — too close
Now (distance 1480)13 — matches OSRS

The arithmetic, kept in a comment in main.ts: with a 38° vertical FOV the horizontal focal length is 334 / (2 × tan 19°) = 485px, so a 128-unit tile at the player's depth covers 128 × 485 / distance pixels. 1480 puts a tile at ~42px.

artLighting and ground texture

  • Ambient dropped 0.68 → 0.55 and a cool bounce term added, so the flat-shaded geometry has readable contrast.
  • Fog pushed back (near 3200 → 4400, far 6400 → 7800) and darkened, killing the washed-out haze.
  • New uGroundDetail fragment-shader path stipples terrain with a per-half-tile hash pattern. It is switched on for terrain draws only and off for models, via renderer.setGroundDetail().
  • The compass reset button now restores the default pitch and zoom, not just the yaw.

Guided intro for a first-time player

A 16-item Getting Started checklist replaces the stub Quest tab. It is tracked server-side, persisted in the save file, ticks off automatically as you play, highlights the next outstanding task in yellow, and spells out how to do it.

  • Definitions live in src/shared/data/tasks.ts (id, name, hint).
  • Player.completeTask() fires once per task and pushes a tasks packet.
  • Skilling tasks hook a single place — the XP_TASKS map inside Player.addXp() — rather than being sprinkled through the action handlers.
  • Other hooks: startDialogue (talk), equipItem (equip), openBank / openShop, ground-item take (loot), eat, togglePrayer, killEntity (kill), and the walk packet.
  • New accounts also get two lines of chat on login: how to walk, and where the checklist is.

Hard-won gotchas (all also written into AGENTS.md)

  • ::tele x y keeps your current plane. This cost real debugging time: after the stairs test the probe was on plane 1, and every later teleport stayed there, so objects "vanished" because world.findObject searches the player's plane. Always write ::tele x y 0.
  • objectScreenPos() must bound-check the viewport. Projecting an off-screen object and clicking it lands on the side panel, which looks exactly like broken picking. I chased a phantom "right-click menus are broken" bug for a while because of this — menus were fine.
  • NPCs wander, so a test that needs to stand next to one cannot trust a spawn coordinate. play-check.ts has stepOntoNpc() which re-locates the nearest match and retries.
  • Aggression is capped by combat level. NpcDef.aggroMaxLevel is 15 for a Dark wizard, so a boosted test account is simply ignored. Reset combat stats before testing aggro.
  • Panel content must fit PANEL.h = 261px. Skills, Prayer, Magic and Getting Started all draw a grid plus a description strip; overflow spills into the bottom tab row at y=466 rather than being clipped.
  • Combat at level 1 is genuinely slow and that is correct. Unarmed, an accurate-style level-1 player has a max hit of 1 and about a 58% hit chance, so a 3hp chicken takes ~10 attacks. Early probe runs looked like "combat is broken" when it was just faithful maths plus bad luck.
  • The blue octagon in the Lumbridge courtyard is the fountain, not a rendering bug.
  • The client is served from dist/client, so npm start serves a stale bundle until you rebuild. Console errors that look like real bugs are often just a stale dist.

Honest status & what's next

This is not a 1:1 replica and cannot be — the real game has twenty years of content. What it is now is a faithful, legible OSRS core loop with no known broken systems.

Known limits

  • Actor and scenery models are low-poly boxes. Recognisable, not RuneScape.
  • Three quests (Cook's Assistant, Sheep Shearer, The Restless Ghost) — the dialogue engine supports more.
  • Map is Lumbridge / Al Kharid / Draynor / Wizards' Tower / the swamp and mines, not all of Gielinor.
  • No trading between players, no Grand Exchange, no minigames.
  • aggroCooldown on Npc is declared and decremented but never set — harmless dead code, could be wired up or removed.

Suggested next steps not started

  1. Better models — actors and scenery are the biggest remaining gap to "looks like OSRS".
  2. More map — extend north toward Varrock, or fill in interiors.
  3. More quests, using the existing SCRIPTS dialogue engine in dialogue.ts.
  4. Player-to-player trading (the right-click entry already exists and says "not available yet").
